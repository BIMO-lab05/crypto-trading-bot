<objective>
Close P21-1: thread the aggregator's real ATR into the `simple_rsi` and `mean_reversion` ensemble legs — **together with** the percent-to-fraction unit contract that makes doing so safe. Also close the two P21-8 capital-literal defaults that live in the same two files.

Purpose: `indicators["ATR"]` is never written today, so two of three ensemble legs size every stop off a hardcoded 2% and `mean_reversion`'s SMA-deviation sub-signals can never fire.

Output: a synthetic ATR `IndicatorSignal` built from `metadata["atr"]` into a **local copy** of the indicator dict, a shared usability predicate, an explicit unit contract on the consuming side, and tests that pin all of it.

## Why the threading and the unit contract are one task and one commit

`simple_rsi_strategy.py:60-69` reads `metadata["atr_pct"]` and guesses the unit with `if atr_pct > 1.0: atr_pct /= 100`. The TA service emits `atr_pct` as a **percent** (`atr.py:91` computes `(atr / current_price) * 100`), and sub-1% hourly ATR is an explicitly modelled regime (`atr.py:94` buckets `atr_pct < 1.0` as LOW volatility). Measured against the running TA service on 2026-08-26:

| Symbol | live `atr_pct` (60m) | `> 1.0` fires? | fraction `simple_rsi` would use | stop distance at the 2.0x mult |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| BTCUSDT | 0.6658 | no | 0.666 | 133% of price -> **negative stop** |
| ETHUSDT | 0.8376 | no | 0.838 | 168% of price -> **negative stop** |
| BNBUSDT | 0.7182 | no | 0.718 | 144% of price -> **negative stop** |
| SOLUSDT | 1.1065 | yes | 0.01107 | 2.2% — correct |
| ADAUSDT | 1.4043 | yes | 0.01404 | 2.8% — correct |

Three of the five tradeable symbols were in the broken branch at measurement time, and they are the three largest by notional. Threading real ATR without the unit contract ships negative stop-losses on every BUY for those symbols. The defect is invisible today **only** because the key is never populated. Splitting these across two tasks or two commits creates a window in which the broken combination can ship. Do not split them.

This is the same class of defect as CLAUDE.md §5's units trap (`max_risk_per_trade` is a fraction; `max_daily_loss_pct` is a percent) — which has shipped once already.

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| technical-analysis ATR payload -> trading-engine risk sizing | A cross-service numeric whose UNIT is not carried in the type; the consumer currently guesses it |
| aggregator indicator dict -> ensemble legs -> voter/regime observers | The same dict object is shared with `observe_regime` and the signal cache; mutating it can put a non-voting indicator into a vote |
