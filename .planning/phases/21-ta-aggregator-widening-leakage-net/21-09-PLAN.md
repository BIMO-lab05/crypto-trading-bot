<objective>
Measure what this phase actually changed, attribute the change per fix, and run the phase gate.

Purpose: three fixes in this phase move trade admission and **they push in opposite directions**. Plan 21-03's ATR threading *increases* admission (it unlocks `mean_reversion`'s `PRICE_BELOW/ABOVE_SMA` sub-signals, worth up to +0.25 confidence and a second firing route) while also *decreasing* it on ATR fetch failure (the shared usability predicate turns `atr.py::_default_response()` into `UNUSABLE_LEVEL` instead of a silent 3% stop). Plans 21-05 and 21-06 *decrease* admission. The net direction is unknown a priori, and a single before/after number would be uninterpretable.

**Do not measure this on live signal pulls.** The live funnel has emitted zero trades since 2026-08-16; the 60m timeframe was HOLD in 2,820 of 2,820 evaluations and MTF consensus resolved HOLD in 90 of 90 recomputed cycles. A live before/after will read "no change" for every fix and prove nothing. A verification step whose success criterion is "signals unchanged" is the failure mode. The live five-symbol pull is **smoke evidence only** — no crash, ATR present in `metadata['atr']`, services healthy.

**No profitability claim.** Report admission counts and signal deltas. Never P&L, never "improved", never "better". Profitability is explicitly not a goal of this phase, and CLAUDE.md §2 forbids any edge claim without DSR/CPCV.

Output: a committed, re-runnable ablation script; a behavior-change evidence record with per-fix attribution; and a phase-gate record.
