`last_updated_at` in Phase 7 (audit table rows with
`last_updated_at_emitter='no'` are the follow-up tickets).

| T-06-05-03 | Denial of Service | Retry button spam | LOW | accept | React Query's `refetch()` is idempotent and gated by its own dedupe; clicking Retry rapidly is harmless. No new rate limit needed at UI; gateway rate-limit middleware covers the per-IP case. |
