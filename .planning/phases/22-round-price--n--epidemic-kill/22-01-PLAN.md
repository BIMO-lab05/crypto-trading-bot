<objective>
Kill the fixed-precision price rounding on the **live ensemble path** (`simple_rsi_strategy.py` SL/TP) and on the two trading-engine serialization sites, then enroll all three files in the existing AST invariant guard so the class cannot reopen.

Purpose: `round(price, 4)` on a sub-$1 asset is the 487d1bd defect class one order of magnitude down. ADA trades near $0.60 with a 0.0001 tick; a 4dp stop is quantized to exactly one tick, so any real sub-tick stop offset is silently destroyed. The live path is leg 1 of the ensemble — these SL/TP values flow into `EnsembleSignal` and become the orders the paper engine fills.

Output: one new regression test, three cleaned source files, guard coverage grown from 6 → 9 files.
</objective>

<classification_rule>

The marker is a claim, not a silencer. Never mark a per-unit price to make the guard pass.
