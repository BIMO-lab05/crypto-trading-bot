- After writing new credentials to Vault, run `subprocess.run(["docker", "compose", "-f", "docker-compose.unified.yml", "restart", "bybit-connector"], check=True, timeout=60)` and wait for the connector to come back healthy (poll /health for up to 30s).
- THEN issue the auth ping. The ping now uses the NEW credentials because bybit-connector re-read them at boot.
- Mitigation T-BC02-CredentialSpill: the new credentials never leave the env-var space; restart preserves them via .env propagation. Log lines must NOT echo credentials (use SecretMaskingFormatter pattern referenced in services/bybit-connector/app/main.py).
