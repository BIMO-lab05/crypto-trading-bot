---

The deployed ensemble earns **under a fifth of its own taker cost**. Any candidate
strategy must beat these numbers by the hurdle multiple before it earns a single
CPCV/DSR run.

Note `net, maker` is *positive* on the 1.5x variant. That is not a passing grade:
the maker ratio is 1.220x, short of the 2x hurdle, and 4 bps covers explicit fees
only — adverse selection and non-fill are real costs this model structurally
cannot express. The headline verdict is and remains the TAKER verdict.
