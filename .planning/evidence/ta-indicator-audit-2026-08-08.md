### 1. Verdict

**The arithmetic is sound; the layer that turns it into votes is not.** Every core calculation that was checked against an independently written reference reproduced it to floating-point noise or better — MACD/SMA/EMA bit-exact (0.000e+00), RSI Wilder to 1e-8, Stochastic to 1e-13, ADX genuine double-smoothed Wilder to 0.004, Keltner/True-Range/linreg exact, no look-ahead anywhere in indicator math, no price-domain rounding outside two named sites. There is no "SMA where Wilder was required", no forming-candle leak, no ADA precision bug in the indicator kernels. What is broken sits entirely above the math: **confidence shaping that is non-monotonic, calibration constants that are asset-dependent, warm-up gates that emit confident numbers from unconverged recursions, unset parameters, and one string-literal contract mismatch that silently disables the volume filter on 65% of bars.** So: these indicators are fit to build on *after* a bounded, cheap repair of the shaping layer — you are not looking at a rewrite. But repairing them is not a plan to reach positive edge, and you should not fund it as one. I measured the ensemble's selection value directly against its own output series, and it is the strongest single result in this document: over the 12,371 scoreable signals, ensemble directional accuracy is 49.13% (reproducing the reported 49.07%), while a constant always-SELL rule on those same bars scores **51.70%** and the unconditional 24 h down-rate across all bars is 51.15% — because all five symbols fell 35–154% in log terms over 2025-08-19 → 2026-08-06. Split by side, BUY scores 47.09% against a 48.30% long base rate and SELL scores 50.75% against a 51.70% short base rate: **the ensemble subtracts roughly one point of accuracy on both sides, symmetrically.** That symmetry is diagnostic. A directional-bias defect (the BB SELL surplus, the Ichimoku bearish tie-breaks) would show as *asymmetric* selection value; it does not. What shows is equal loss on both sides, which is the fingerprint of noise and mis-weighting, not of a thumb on one scale — and it is consistent with the confidence-shaping defects below being real contributors while none of them is the cause.

### 3. What plausibly moves the 49.07%, and what does not

**Plausible contributors, in the measurement, ranked:** #1 (validator inert on 65% of bars), #2 (20% of votes wrong on the heaviest leg), #3 and #4 (the two non-monotone confidence curves, which mis-weight the highest-information bars), #5/#6/#7 (the counter-trend gate is simultaneously mislabelled ~5%, calibrated per-asset by accident, and fails open on any error), #8, #11.

The mechanism they share is the one the measurement fingerprints: they corrupt **weight and filtering**, not direction, and they do so on both sides. That matches the −1pp symmetric selection loss on BUY and SELL alike.

**Real but too small to explain chance-level performance — say so and do not inflate:** the Bollinger `ddof=1` defect (bands 2.6% too wide; 1.1–1.6% of signals suppressed, one-directionally only); the volume inclusive-average window (~4% tier flips, ~1.3% confirm/reject flips, and a convention TradingView shares — a documentation defect more than a math one); the Ichimoku bearish tie-breaks (#10, 0.13–0.67% of votes); the Ichimoku cloud off-by-one (#9, ~1.2%). Each is genuine. None is a smoking gun.

**Explicitly not contributing, and deliberately not ranked:**
- *Disabled legs* — both SQZMOM implementations (`signal_aggregator.py:744/746`), RSI divergence. The divergence detector's 68–71% false-positive rate is real, and its "stuck at 0.20 confidence" disable reason is fully explained by it (broken pivot matching leaves both divergences `None` in ~90% of windows → `HOLD, 0.2`). Fix before re-enabling; it is not in the number.
- *Latent with zero measured occurrences* — RSI zero-avg-loss inversion (returns 0 where 100 is required; 0/17,500 bars), Stochastic zero-range %K (returns 0 where 50 is conventional; 0/17,494 windows), ATR flat-series (stop == entry, `volatility: LOW, confidence 0.8`; longest zero-TR run is 0 bars). All three are inverted extremes waiting on a stalled feed or a low-liquidity symbol, and the universe is widening to 14 symbols. Fix them before that lands, not now.
- *Dead code* — `rsi_divergence.calculate_with_confirmation` (no caller; its pin-bar detector labels 22% of bars bullish, a third of them shooting-star shaped, and every doji), `multi_indicator_strategy.apply_filters` (a second, stricter trend gate that never runs), `macd.calculate_series` (unmasked contaminated leading rows), `trading-engine/app/multi_timeframe.py`.
- *Off-by-one warm-up guards* unreachable over HTTP: Stochastic %D (17 vs 18 bars), SQZMOM basic (21 vs 38) and enhanced (25 vs 30, and it coerces the NaN to a fabricated `0.0`), `limit=30` returning HTTP 500.
- *One genuine look-ahead* — `sqzmom_enhanced.py:951` normalises each row by the whole-frame max. It has no current consumer (endpoints return only the last row), so it is a loaded gun in an emitted column, not an active loss.

### 4. Verified correct — this bounds where the problem is not

This is the most decision-relevant section, because it eliminates the hypotheses that would have justified a rewrite.

- **Core arithmetic, against independently written references, on 8,759 bars × 2 symbols:** MACD line/signal/histogram **0.000e+00** at both 5-35-5 and 8-17-9. SMA(20)/SMA(21) **0.000e+00**. EMA(20)/EMA(21) **0.000e+00**. RSI Wilder **2.1e-08**. Stochastic %K/%D **8.5e-14 / 1.1e-13**. ADX/+DI/−DI **0.0010–0.0038** (inside the output's own 2-dp rounding). True Range **exactly 0.0**, including the bar-0 convention. Keltner Channels **0.000e+00**. Volume ratio **float-identical**. SQZMOM linear regression **2e-12 / 4e-17**.
- **Wilder is genuinely Wilder** where it is required. RSI uses `ewm(alpha=1/period, adjust=False)`. ADX is genuinely **double**-smoothed, no single-pass shortcut. Keltner correctly uses an SMA of TR (LazyBear's definition), so the Wilder-vs-SMA trap does not apply there. The one place α is wrong is ATR (#13), and seeding was explicitly ruled out as the cause.
- **No look-ahead in any indicator.** Zero `shift(-…)` across `technical-analysis/app`, `trading-engine/app` and `backtesting/`. Every rolling/ewm window is trailing. Pivot detection only reports a pivot once the right-hand bars exist. The forming candle is dropped twice — market-data before storage (`fetcher.py:191`, `scheduler.py:193`) and TA defensively (`fetcher.py:222-230`) — and the replay's `frozen_time` pins it to bar close. *This closes the squeeze agent's open question; it is answered, not outstanding.*
- **No price-domain rounding in the indicator kernels.** The PRICE-01/02 class is absent from `rsi.py`, `stochastic.py`, `rsi_divergence.py`, `macd.py`, `moving_averages.py`, `trend_filter.py`, `bollinger_bands.py`, `atr.py`, `adx.py`, `ichimoku.py`. Only `round(confidence, 2)` and dimensionless index values. `round(rsi_value, 2)` at `indicator_service.py:58` is **not** a defect (0–100 oscillator) and should not be re-flagged. The only two real sites are `indicator_service.py:84-86` (#14) and `squeeze_momentum.py:541` (ADA momentum, 51 sign-destroying bars/yr, no live consumer).
- **ADA is not a precision victim in the kernels.** Every numeric check reproduced on ADA to 15+ significant figures, equal to or better than BTC.
- **No division-by-zero and no crash** on flat/degenerate series anywhere in the group: MACD, SMA, EMA, TrendFilter, BB, ATR, ADX and both SQZMOM implementations all degrade to neutral without exception. Several return a *wrong* neutral value (see §3 latents), but none throws.
- **Ichimoku Chikou is exactly right** — `-displacement - 1`, divergence 0.0000000000 on all 8,560 windows on both symbols. It is the witness that convicts the cloud index at `:171`.

### 6. Disagreements and open items

- **Three agents vs. the harness (resolved, above).** Best-evidenced: the file chain plus the exact 12,400 − 13 = 12,387 arithmetic. Their *defect* findings stand; only their contamination verdicts invert.
- **"Does the fetcher drop the forming candle?" (resolved).** The squeeze agent flagged it unaudited and warned it could dwarf everything. The volume agent answered it: yes it drops it, by wall clock (`fetcher.py:222-230`), and the replay pins that clock. The residual defect is the wall-clock mechanism and its fail-open branch — no staleness ceiling, so a stalled collector serves week-old candles confidently — not a missing drop.
- **Open, not mine to resolve — escalate to t7-stops / t8-enstops.** The volatility agent's brief said ATR now feeds authoritative ensemble stops. A repo-wide grep found only three hits on `atr_stop_loss`: `multi_strategy_ensemble.py:208-209` **reads** it defaulting to `current_price`, and the sole **writer** is `scripts/backtest_ensemble.py:179`, which writes a hardcoded `price*0.98`. If that holds, every ensemble trade carries a zero-width stop at entry — larger than anything in this document, and unverified. Do not let it die between agents.
- **Two ATR implementations disagree with each other and with Wilder** (`atr.py:90` span-14 EMA vs `atr_stops.py:159` flat SMA of TR). Reconcile before any cross-service stop unification.
- **Structural, upstream of four separate calibration findings:** the confidences these indicators emit are not probabilities and are not comparable across indicators or across symbols, yet `aggregator_core` sums them as weights and gates on a single global `signal_confidence_threshold=0.6`. Findings #3, #4, #6 and #11 are all symptoms of that one mismatch. Whoever owns the aggregator should own it as a finding in its own right.
