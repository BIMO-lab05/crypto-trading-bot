## 2. Rebuild gate

### Command

The build and the recreate were run as two steps rather than one
`up -d --build --force-recreate`, for two recorded reasons:

1. The single combined command walks the dependency graph and began rebuilding
   `bybit-connector` and `market-data`, then died on the known WSL2 Docker
   Desktop credential-helper failure:
   `<3>WSL (430712) ERROR: UtilAcceptVsock:271: accept4 failed 110` /
   `error listing credentials - err: exit status 1`. The documented bypass is to
   point Docker at an empty config so no helper is invoked.
2. `--no-deps` on the recreate keeps `postgres` and `timescaledb` untouched. This
   host has a recurring suspend-related bind-mount hazard that kills those two
   with `exit=127 "not a directory"`, and there was no reason to restart them.

```
$ mkdir -p $S/dockercfg && echo '{"auths":{}}' > $S/dockercfg/config.json
$ DOCKER_CONFIG=$S/dockercfg DOCKER_BUILDKIT=0 \

## 3. Live smoke — labelled smoke, not behaviour proof

**This is smoke evidence only.** The live funnel has emitted zero trades since
2026-08-16 and the 60m timeframe resolved HOLD in every recomputed cycle, so a
live before/after would read "no change" for every fix and prove nothing. A
success criterion of "signals unchanged" is the failure mode this phase was
warned about. What the smoke proves is narrow and stated as such: no crash, ATR
reaches the engine, and the stop distances it produces are sane.

Both containers were force-recreated and then left **more than 30 seconds**
before the pull, so the 30-second TA kline cache could not serve the pre-restart
frame.

Route: `GET http://localhost:8005/api/v1/signals/{symbol}` → `signal.metadata.atr`.

```

- **No 5xx** — all five returned HTTP 200.
- **`metadata['atr']` present** for all five, with a full payload
  (`atr`, `atr_pct`, `stop_loss_long/short`, `take_profit_long/short`,
  `volatility`, `confidence`, `risk_reward_ratio`).
- **Every stop distance is a sub-10% fraction of price** — 1.12% to 2.79%, on
  both the `simple_rsi` route (`atr_pct/100 × ATR_STOP_MULT`) and the
  `multi_indicator` route (`_atr_levels` reading `stop_loss_long` verbatim).

**This is the concrete proof that the P21-1 unit trap is closed on live data.**
The last two columns show what the pre-phase magnitude guess
(`if atr_pct > 1.0: atr_pct /= 100`) would have produced from these same
readings. **BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT and BNBUSDT all carry `atr_pct < 1.0` today**, so
all three fall in the broken branch and would have produced **negative stop
prices** — −9,283, −1,368 and −102. SOL and ADA are above 1.0 and would have been
correct by luck of which side of the constant their reading fell on. Three of the
five, and the three largest by notional, exactly as 21-03 measured on 2026-08-26.
