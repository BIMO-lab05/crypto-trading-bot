## Key finding — harness validates the wrong strategy

The walk-forward harness exercises `phase1_strategy_prod`
(`backtesting/run_walk_forward.py:105`), a tight single-strategy
template: RSI < 20 / > 80 + EMA20 alignment + ADX ≥ 20 + TrendFilter
non-opposing + VolumeConfirmation + ATR stops. On 180 days of recent
data across all 5 symbols, those conditions never coalesce, so the
strategy fires zero trades.

Live system runs **ensemble mode** (per `/api/trading/status →
strategy_mode: "ensemble"`). The ensemble aggregator combines 8–9
weighted votes (RSI, MACD, Stochastic, RSI-divergence, SMA, EMA,
Ichimoku, Bollinger, Squeeze-Momentum) with consensus-threshold
voting plus multi-timeframe blending. Lower bar than phase1's
unanimous-agreement template — and observable in production: 31
closed trades in last 30 days (per `/api/trading/statistics`, restored
post `bd23a3c` fix), 2 wins / 24 losses / 6.45% win rate, ~$25 net
loss on $100 starting capital.

**Consequence:** ADR-013 §B's "OOS Sharpe > 1.0 + DSR > 0.95 before
re-arming" gate has been measuring a strategy that does not run in
production. Previous 2025-12-06 results (OOS Sharpe -7.48 / -8.18 /
-8.53 on ADA / BNB / SOL) were against the same phase1 template —
also not informative for ensemble edge.

## What this run does and does NOT prove

- **Does prove:** The phase1 single-strategy template is dormant under
  current default params on the last 180 days. Either the gate is too
  tight (RSI<20 + EMA agreement + ADX>=20 + trend non-opposite + volume
  confirm + non-counter-direction is unanimous-vote on six legs) or
  the regime simply hasn't presented setups. Either way, phase1 alone
  has no edge to gate against in this window — no IS Sharpe, no OOS
  Sharpe, no claim to evaluate.
- **Does NOT prove:** Anything about the live ensemble's edge or
  lack thereof. The harness does not exercise the production code
  path. The 31 live trades that closed −$25 in 30 days are still the
  only signal we have on ensemble performance, and they say *no edge*
  — but via a sample size (31) too small to reject the null at any
  reasonable significance level.

## Recommended next steps

1. **Keep the bot paused.** No evidence-backed reason to re-arm. The
   live sample is small (31 trades) and net negative; the harness
   gives no positive signal.
2. **Build an ensemble walk-forward harness** that exercises
   `EnhancedAggregator` + `Voter` + multi-timeframe blender on the
   same data. Acceptance gate inherits from ADR-013 §B
   (OOS Sharpe > 1.0, OOS/IS ≥ 0.60, DSR > 0.95). Without this, the
   pre-arm checklist is hollow for the deployed strategy.
3. **Diagnose the phase1 dormancy.** Tighten or loosen the gate to
   produce non-zero trade counts and see whether phase1 *can* show
   edge on this regime, or is a strict-gate dead end. Useful as a
   floor-of-no-edge sanity check.
4. **Don't rely on the 31-trade live sample as edge evidence.** With
   2 wins it's well within "anyone can get 2 winners by chance"
   territory. Treat as no-edge-detected until a harness-grade
   evaluation passes.
5. **Treat the 12-day pre-arm-without-evidence gap as a process
   incident.** ADR-013 §B was supposed to gate re-arming; on
   2026-05-19 the bot un-paused without re-running the harness or
   noting that the harness isn't even measuring the right strategy.

```
    --symbol <SYM> \
    --days 180 \
    --folds 4 \
    --is-frac 0.75 \
    --ack-mixed-data \
    --n-trials 10
```

`--ack-mixed-data` bypasses the testnet-flip taint guard
(2026-04-25 mid-day flip). Oldest fold's IS slice begins
2025-08-24 — well before the flip — so its candles are
testnet-priced. Newer folds (1–3) progressively shift to mostly
mainnet data. The 0-trade outcome is consistent across all folds
including the cleanest (fold 3, 2025-12-04 → 2026-05-19), so the
testnet contamination is not the cause of the no-signal verdict.
