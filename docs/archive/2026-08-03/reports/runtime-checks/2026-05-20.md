## Diagnosis

**FLAT / NO TRADES** — bot armed but not firing. Five carry-over days with zero fills visible on disk. Equity drift +$0.030 over 8.25h is rounding from a snapshot writer that records portfolio value but writes `pnl=0` regardless of price moves; not a real P&L delta.

Why bot does not trade — ordered by likelihood:

1. **Risk-metrics circuit breaker tripping on phantom data and blocking entries.** Daily loss reads of -6% / -7% / -2507% are not coming from real positions (there are none) but from somewhere — stale Redis cache, mock fixture, or a metric pipeline reading the wrong column. Breaker enters OPEN state, 300s cooldown, and auto-trader respects the breaker. 68 trips in 24h means most of the window is breaker-OPEN. Commits `71c0c13` and `ba39507` claim to fix this; they did not.
2. **Signal aggregator timing out under 11-symbol fan-out.** 81 signal-eval timeouts in 24h plus 59 connection drops. When the trading-engine returns nothing for SOLUSDT/ETHUSDT/etc., auto-trader has no signal to act on — same effect as a flat HOLD vote.
3. **Live ensemble strategy fails its own walk-forward gate 0/5.** PF mean below 1.20 on BTC, OOS/IS ratio inverted on SOL, ADA produces 0 trades on the OOS window. If signals are arriving cleanly, the aggregator's verdict is HOLD because no edge.
4. **Snapshot writer broken on UPSERT** — even when a trade IS closed, portfolio-manager logs do not show a `pnl != 0` row because the snapshot path keeps hitting the ON CONFLICT bug at 00:00:00 UTC. P&L could be happening and we'd see `pnl=0`. Less likely (the "fetched 0 positions" line is independent and corroborates zero activity), but adds opacity to any future fill.

Best guess: auto-trader is gagged by (1) and producing HOLDs from (3). (2) intermittently blanks the signals on top of that. (4) means we couldn't see fills cleanly even if they happened.

## Recommendations

### 1. Wrong now (fix this week)

1. **Trace the phantom -2507.74% daily-loss reading.** Start at `services/risk-metrics-service/app/risk_engine.py` — find where `daily_pnl` is sourced. Likely a Redis key fed by either a unit-test fixture or a percentage-vs-fraction units bug (`pnl * 100` applied twice somewhere). Until this is right, the breaker is gaslighting the auto-trader. Commit `ba39507` was supposed to do this — write a unit test that fails at -2507% and prove the fix.
2. **Restart trading-engine + portfolio-manager + risk-metrics-service after applying recent fixes.** Commits `353a4d3` (leverage), `e03291a` (UPSERT), `71c0c13` and `ba39507` (CB) are all in main but the failure modes they target still appear in today's logs. Run `docker compose -f docker-compose.unified.yml up -d --force-recreate trading-engine portfolio-manager risk-metrics-service` and re-audit after 1h.
3. **Fix the duplicate scheduler in portfolio-manager.** Every `Periodic Price Update` job logs twice within ~5 ms with the same `next_run`. APScheduler likely instantiated twice in `app/scheduler/performance_snapshot.py` — singleton it. Side-effect: snapshot writes happen twice and both hit the unique constraint.
4. **Fix DNS-fail loop on `ml-prediction` and `sentiment-analysis` in api-gateway.** Frontend gate `7897201` only covered the React-side calls. The gateway-internal aggregator/health-poll path still resolves those service names. Either short-circuit those calls when `ENABLE_ML_PREDICTIONS=false` / `ENABLE_SENTIMENT_ANALYSIS=false`, or remove the dead routes from gateway config.
5. **Align `MAX_DAILY_LOSS_PCT` between trading-engine (10.0) and risk-metrics-service circuit-breaker (5.0).** They are working against each other today. Pick one — 5% looks correct for paper mode, drop trading-engine's `.env` line to match — then redocument in CLAUDE.md.

### 2. Missing (capabilities the bot needs before it should fire a real order)

1. **DSR/PSR gate on auto-trader start, not just on ML re-enable.** Walk-forward gate failed 0/5 today and yesterday. Auto-trader still boots armed. Add a preflight that reads the most recent `backtesting/results/wf_ensemble_*/` summary, refuses to arm if no symbol passes gate, and emits a notification. Right now the gate is documentation, not enforcement.
2. **Alert when CB trips ≥ 3 times in 5 minutes.** 68 trips in 24h, operator notices via log audit instead of paging. Wire into notification-service. Treat sustained CB-OPEN as paging-worthy, since it means the auto-trader has been off for hours of supposed market exposure.
3. **Order-vs-position reconciliation job.** Trading-engine `/trades/history` hit 272× today, portfolio-manager `Fetched 0 positions` every minute. If the two ever disagree the bot loses money silently. Add a once-an-hour job that diffs trading-engine trade ledger against portfolio-manager positions, alerts on mismatch.
4. **Live-only snapshot path.** Current performance-history writer ignores `pnl` field (always 0). Cannot diagnose P&L from logs. Either compute `pnl` from value delta vs. previous snapshot, or surface realized P&L from the trades ledger as a separate column.

### 3. Improve (anchored to today's numbers)

1. **Drop the 6 unauthorised symbols (AVAX, LINK, DOT, MATIC, ARB, OP) from the live signal loop** OR write an ADR adding them to the validated set. They consume signal-eval CPU and contribute to the 81 timeouts. Project rule says BTC/ETH/SOL/BNB/ADA only.
2. **ADAUSDT produced 0 trades on 150 OOS bars** in today's walk-forward. Either retune the regime modifier so ADA's aggregator threshold passes occasionally, or remove ADA from the live universe until the strategy generates ADA signals. Today it is a CPU tax for nothing.
3. **Update CLAUDE.md "9-indicator voting" to "13-indicator" or document which 4 are wired off.** Stale doc means future audits chase a number that does not exist.
4. **Wire walk-forward output into the audit cycle.** Today's wf result is on disk (`backtesting/results/wf_ensemble_2026-05-20/`), but the auto-trader does not check it on boot, and CLAUDE.md's "DSR > 0.95 acceptance gate" applies only to ML re-enable, not to the live ensemble.
