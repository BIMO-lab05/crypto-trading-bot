## Maker fill model (frozen)

- Maker fee per side: `backtesting/backtest_engine.py:153-155` — `bybit_maker_fee`
  = 0.020% (+2 bps), a CHARGE not a rebate, per `_BYBIT_PERP_FEES.maker`.
- Entry limit at signal price; FILLS ONLY IF the next bar TRADES THROUGH the
  level (bar low < limit for LONG, bar high > limit for SHORT — strict, not
  touch).
- Unfilled after 1 bar => missed trade; missed count reported per variant.
- Exits: stop = taker fee; take-profit/time exit = maker fee, same
  trade-through rule, fallback to taker market exit at the scheduled exit bar
  if the maker exit does not fill.
- Slippage table unchanged (`config.py:16-25`, `SLIPPAGE_BPS`).
